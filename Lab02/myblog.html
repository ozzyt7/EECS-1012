Animated Movies That You Should Watch

How To Train Your Dragon

How to Train Your Dragon is a heartwarming tale of an unlikely friendship between a young Viking, Hiccup, and a rare dragon, Toothless. Set in a world where dragons are feared enemies, the film beautifully explores themes of courage, understanding, and challenging societal norms. The stunning animation and captivating score bring the vibrant world of Berk to life. Hiccup's journey from an awkward misfit to a brave hero resonates with audiences of all ages. Ultimately, the film celebrates the power of empathy and the transformative impact of seeing the world through another's eyes.

Shrek

Shrek is a timeless animated film that redefined fairy tales with humor and heart. Released in 2001, this DreamWorks classic tells the story of an unlikely hero—an ogre named Shrek—who embarks on a quest to save Princess Fiona, only to find true love and friendship along the way. The movie brilliantly subverts traditional fairy tale tropes, offering witty dialogue, memorable characters like Donkey, and a powerful message about self-acceptance. With its groundbreaking animation and iconic soundtrack, Shrek remains a beloved film that continues to entertain audiences of all ages.

Spider-Man: Across The Spider-Verse

Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse is a visually stunning sequel that expands the multiverse concept introduced in the first film. Following Miles Morales, it dives deeper into the complexities of being Spider-Man across different dimensions. The film is a blend of breathtaking animation, heart-pounding action, and emotional storytelling, showcasing diverse Spider-People with unique styles and narratives. Themes of identity, responsibility, and sacrifice are explored as Miles faces new challenges and discovers the true meaning of heroism. With its bold visuals and compelling plot, the movie sets a new standard for animated superhero films.
